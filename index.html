About 312 Stages

312 Stages is a Chicago-area staging rental company. Our crew has worked in live events and entertainment for over 15 years.

We've installed decks and stages for presidential appearances, rock concerts, Fortune 500 conferences, and university theatre. Whatever the site throws at you, we'll find a way to build it.

How a quote works

  1. 01

    Date and venue

    Send the event date, load-in window, and the venue or city.

  2. 02

    Size and height

    An approximate footprint and finished deck height. Sketches and CAD are welcome.

  3. 03

    Add-ons

    Ramps, stairs, guardrail, delivery, and install crew, if you need them.

  4. 04

    We confirm

    We review the request, check availability, and come back with pricing.
